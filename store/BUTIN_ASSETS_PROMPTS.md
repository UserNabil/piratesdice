# Butin du Jour — prompts de génération d'assets

Tous les assets sont des **icônes carrées, fond transparent, contour blanc autocollant**,
dans le style du jeu. Colle d'abord le **socle de style**, puis le sujet.

## Socle de style (à préfixer à CHAQUE prompt)

```
Mobile game icon, bold cartoon pirate treasure style, thick dark outline,
glossy hand-painted shading, rich saturated colors, purple-and-gold palette,
subtle inner glow, die-cut sticker with a clean white border, centered on a
fully transparent background, square 1:1, high detail, no text, no watermark.
```

## Coffres & monnaies

1. **Coffre de la semaine (gros lot)** — `A big ornate pirate treasure chest, purple wood body with heavy gold banding and a skull lock, lid slightly open with warm golden light and coins spilling out.`
2. **Tas d'or (butin quotidien)** — `A generous stack of shiny gold coins, a golden crown coin on top catching the light, one cartoon die leaning against the pile.`
3. **Tas de pièces maudites** — `A stack of eerie silver coins engraved with skulls, faint teal cursed glow, one coin standing on edge showing a crossbones face.`
4. **Pack de bonus ×3** — `Three stacked glowing power-up tokens tied with a rope, each token showing a simple dice-action glyph (reroll arrows), an amber energy aura.`

## Parures de dés à produire (toujours la face « 5 » pour rester lisibles en collection)

5. **Dés d'émeraude** — `A single cartoon die showing the face "5", carved from polished emerald-green gem with gold pips and edges, deep green inner glow.`
6. **Dés d'obsidienne** — `A single die face "5" in glossy black obsidian with molten-orange cracks and gold pips, volcanic ember glow.`
7. **Dés de corail** — `A single die face "5" made of pink-orange sea coral with tiny barnacles and gold pips, underwater sunlit tint.`
8. **Dés de tempête** — `A single die face "5" of storm-grey stone crackling with electric-blue lightning veins and gold pips.`
9. **Dés de jade** — `A single die face "5" carved from imperial jade, subtle marbling, gold pips, soft green translucency.`
10. **Dés d'ivoire** — `A single die face "5" of aged ivory bone with fine scrimshaw etching and dark pips.`
11. **Dés royaux** — `A single die face "5" of deep royal-purple velvet-stone with a tiny gold crown emblem and gold pips.`

## Gravures (motifs) à produire (garder un dé neutre, ne changer que le motif gravé)

12. **Gravure des vagues** — `A neutral cartoon die whose top face is engraved with a stylised curling ocean wave, gold inlaid lines, transparent elsewhere.`
13. **Gravure de la boussole** — `A neutral cartoon die engraved with a pirate compass rose on its face, gold inlaid lines.`
14. **Gravure des chaînes** — `A neutral cartoon die engraved with wrapped iron chains across its face, gold inlaid highlights.`
15. **Gravure de la couronne** — `A neutral cartoon die engraved with a small ornate crown on its face, gold inlaid lines.`

## Statut

16. **Badge de série (streak)** — `A small round emblem with a stylised flame and a number banner, gold rim, for showing consecutive-day streaks.`

---

Cible technique : PNG, ~512×512 minimum, fond transparent (alpha). Redimensionnés
ensuite comme les parures existantes (voir `www/dice/img/` : `cap_*.png`, `S0*`).
