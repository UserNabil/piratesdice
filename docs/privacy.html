The Pirate's Dice — Privacy Policy

Application com.nabil.piratesdice · last updated 20 August 2026

This game is small and so is this policy. It says exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and how to make it disappear.

What the game keeps

DataWhyWhere
Your Play Games player ID (or, if you play as a guest, a random identifier generated on your device) It is your account: it carries your captain, your coins and your ranking The game server
Your display nameSo other captains can see who they are playing The game server
Match results, coins, Elo rating, items bought in the in-game shop The game itselfThe game server
Chosen language, sound and motion settingsTo keep your preferences Your device only — never sent anywhere

What the game does not collect

No email address. No phone number. No contacts, photos, files or location. No advertising identifier. There is no advertising in the app, and no analytics service of any kind. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Signing in with Google

Signing in uses Google Play Games. Google gives the game a player identifier and a display name — not your Google account, not your email address. The app never sees your password. If you decline, or if Play Games is unavailable, the game creates a guest account tied to your device instead.

How it travels, and how long it stays

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS / WSS). Data is kept for as long as the account exists, and is deleted immediately when you ask.

Deleting your data

In the app: ⚙ Settings → Erase my data and account. This permanently deletes your captain, coins, ranking and items. Past matches keep their scores for the other players, without any link to you.

You may also write to the address below; deletion is done within 30 days.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat and no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Politique de confidentialité (français)

Le jeu conserve : votre identifiant de joueur Play Games (ou, en mode invité, un identifiant tiré au hasard sur votre appareil), votre nom affiché, et vos résultats de parties — pièces, classement, objets achetés dans la boutique du jeu. La langue, le son et les commandes au mouvement restent sur votre téléphone et ne sont envoyés nulle part.

Le jeu ne collecte ni adresse e-mail, ni téléphone, ni contacts, ni photos, ni position. Aucune publicité, aucun outil de mesure d'audience, aucun partage avec des tiers.

La connexion passe par Google Play Games : Google transmet un identifiant de joueur et un nom affiché — pas votre compte Google, pas votre adresse. Tout circule chiffré (HTTPS / WSS).

Effacer vos données : dans l'application, ⚙ Réglages → « Effacer mes données et mon compte ». La suppression est immédiate et définitive. Vous pouvez aussi écrire à n.ouldterki@gmail.com : effacement sous 30 jours.

Política de privacidad (español)

El juego guarda: tu identificador de jugador de Play Games (o, en modo invitado, un identificador aleatorio generado en tu dispositivo), tu nombre visible y tus resultados — monedas, clasificación y objetos de la tienda del juego. Borrar tus datos: ⚙ Ajustes → «Borrar mis datos y mi cuenta», o escribe a n.ouldterki@gmail.com.
